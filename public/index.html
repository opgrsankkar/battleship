Play Battleship Online — Free, No Download

Battleship Online is a free, browser-based version of the classic naval strategy game. Hide your fleet on a 10×10 grid, then take turns firing at your opponent's hidden ships until one fleet is sunk. Play solo against a smart computer AI or challenge a friend to real-time 2-player online multiplayer — no download, no sign-up, and no ads. It runs instantly on desktop and mobile.

How to Play Battleship Online

  1. Pick a mode — battle the computer (Easy, Medium, or Hard) or start a multiplayer room to play with a friend.
  2. Place your fleet — position your five ships on the grid. Press Rotate (R) to flip a ship, or hit Random for an instant layout.
  3. Take turns firing — click a square on your opponent's grid to fire. A hit is marked in red, a miss in grey.
  4. Sink every ship — the first admiral to sink the entire enemy fleet wins the battle.

Game Features

  • Single-player vs AI with three difficulty levels — from random shots to probability-based hunting.
  • Online 2-player multiplayer — create a room and share the code or link to play a friend in real time.
  • Free and instant — no account, no install, no download required.
  • Plays anywhere — works in any modern browser on desktop, phone, or tablet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Battleship Online free to play?

Yes. It is completely free with no sign-up, no ads, and no download required — just open the page and play.

Can I play Battleship online with a friend?

Yes. Choose Multiplayer, create a room, and share the room code or link. You play a live 2-player game against each other in real time.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. Battleship Online runs entirely in your web browser, so there is nothing to download or install on desktop or mobile.

Can I play Battleship against the computer?

Yes. Single-player mode lets you battle an AI opponent with Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels.

Does Battleship Online work on mobile?

Yes. The game is designed to work on phones and tablets as well as desktop browsers.
